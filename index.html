    What the AI actually does

    Conversational, patient, and completely spoken.

    01

    Regional language detection

    Identifies regional accents and dialects automatically.

    02

    Zero-keyword matching

    Understands everyday problem descriptions without formal scheme titles.

    03

    Simplified explanations

    Explains scheme rules in simple, compassionate conversational terms.

    04

    Spoken document checklist

    Tells callers the exact papers required before they make a trip to town.

    05

    Local office guidance

    Directs the caller to their local Gram Panchayat, Taluk office, or Seva Kendra.

    06

    SMS summary backup

    Sends a short text receipt to their keypad phone for reference.

    Under the hood, kept simple

    Voice telephony combined with open AI APIs.

    IVR

    Telephony Gateway

    Connects inbound calls using Twilio or Exotel infrastructure.

    ASR

    Indic Speech Recognition

    Bhashini / Google STT transcribes regional speech in real time.

    LLM

    Intelligence Engine

    Retrieves scheme eligibility and drafts simple vernacular answers.

    TTS

    Voice Synthesis

    Reads instructions back clearly in the caller's spoken dialect.
